# Fieldwork & Observational Notes

## 1. Place & Observation Window
- **Location:** Mahender Dry Cleaners & Press, Gate No. 2, College Hostel Road (approx. 50m from Hostel 3).
- **Date & Window:** Saturday, November 21, 2026, 08:15 AM – 08:45 AM (30 minutes of direct observational fieldwork).
- **Field Evidence Photos (stored in `photos/`):**
  1. `photos/shop_counter_clutter.jpg` (Timestamped: 08:22 AM — 14 uncollected pressed shirts wrapped in Hindi newspaper hanging across double overhead wires above the ironing table).

## 2. Conversation with the Person
- **Name:** Mahender Kumar
- **Role:** Sole Proprietor & Ironing Operator (running this shop for 7 years).
- **Three Exact Things He Said (Direct Quotes):**
  1. *"Bhaiya, subah-subah ladke aate hain interview ka rona leke ki unka formal shirt kahan hai, jabki 4 din se yahan pada dhool kha raha tha."*
  2. *"Agar main phone karne baithunga, to iron thandi ho jayegi aur koyla ya bijli dono barbad hoga. Ek din me 40 ladko ko call karna namumkin hai."*
  3. *"Paisa to tabhi milega jab kapde leke jayenge. 15-20 jodi kapde latke rehte hain to meri 500-600 rupaye ki daily dihadi phas jati hai."*
- **One Thing That Surprised Me:**
  - I originally assumed students delayed picking up clothes because they forgot or were busy in classes. Mahender revealed that **students deliberately use his shop as an extended, dust-free closet storage space** because hostel almirahs are cramped and clothes get wrinkled inside wardrobes! They only come when an interview or party is scheduled in the next 2 hours.

## 3. Data Collection Methodology
- I sat on the blue plastic stool right next to Mahender's pressing table between 08:15 AM and 09:15 AM.
- Transcribed 22 consecutive ticket entries directly from his physical paper ledger (`data.csv`).
- For each entry, recorded the assigned Token ID, customer name, hostel room number, mobile number, count of garments, service requested (ironing @ ₹10, wash+iron @ ₹20, dry clean @ ₹80-90), drop timestamp, promised pickup timestamp, actual pickup status, and total unpaid amount.
